**Registration** for the 2026–27 season is done through IceReg. Use the button on this page, or find the Edmonton Speed Skating Association under *Find › Clubs* on [icereg.ca](https://icereg.ca/#!/).

**Rental skates** are offered by ESSA for an extra fee. You can pay the fee with your registration by choosing the *Skate Rental* option, which includes an additional $100 deposit. Contact [rentals@edmontonspeedskating.ca](mailto:rentals@edmontonspeedskating.ca) for more information.

A **$200 volunteer bond per family** is added to your invoice automatically. It keeps volunteer participation fair so the club runs smoothly, and it's refunded once your volunteer hours are complete. There are many positions available and plenty of chances over the season to fulfil your hours.

We look forward to seeing you throughout the 2026–27 season!
